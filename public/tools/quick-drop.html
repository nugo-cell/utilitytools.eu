Quick Drop

Send text, links or a short note between two devices — phone to laptop, work PC to home PC. Open a temporary room, scan the QR code on the other device, and paste. Data goes directly device-to-device over encrypted WebRTC; nothing is stored.

Start or join a drop room

How it works: one device creates a room and shows a QR code. The other device scans it (or types the code). Once both are connected, anything you send appears instantly on the other screen. Close the tab and the room disappears.
