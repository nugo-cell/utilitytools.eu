FAQ

Is anything uploaded? No. PDF assembly happens in your browser using pdf-lib.

Which image formats are supported? JPG and PNG are embedded directly. Other formats (WebP, BMP, GIF) are auto-converted to JPG before embedding.

How do I change the order? Drag any image card to a new position. The number badge updates instantly.

Can I include one image per page or multiple? One image per page. To put several images on one page, combine them in our image editor first.
